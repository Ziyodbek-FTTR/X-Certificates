English Proficiency Exam

IELTS

IELTS is an English language proficiency test with four sections: Listening, Reading, Writing and Speaking.

Test Sections4 Sections
Test TypesAcademic & GT
Score ScaleBand 0–9
Writing2 Tasks

Exam Overview

Official IELTS information

Test Types

  • IELTS Academic
  • IELTS General Training

Listening and Speaking are the same in both tests. Reading and Writing are different.

Test Duration

Listening Approx. 30 min
Reading 60 min
Writing 60 min
Speaking 11–14 min

Listening

4 parts and 40 questions. The recordings are heard once only.

Reading

40 questions and 60 minutes. The Reading test differs between Academic and General Training.

Writing

2 tasks in 60 minutes. Academic Task 1 uses visual information; General Training Task 1 is a letter. Task 2 is an essay in both versions.

Speaking

11–14 minutes and 3 parts: questions about familiar topics, an individual long turn, and a longer discussion.
